Construction IT · Reno, Nevada

IT support for Reno construction companies.

Your work happens on jobsites, but the bids, plans, payroll and email live in the office. Founding Fathers IT makes the two work together for contractors and trades businesses in Reno, and makes sure a lost tablet or a departing foreman doesn't turn into a security problem.

Office and jobsite, connected

Crews need the current plans and estimates on site, and the office needs the field to stay in touch. We set up secure remote access, shared folders in Microsoft 365, and devices that just work for your people in the truck.

  • Secure remote access from the jobsite
  • Shared plans, bids and estimates
  • Laptops, tablets and phones for crews
  • Microsoft 365 email and file sharing

Locked down when people leave

Construction crews turn over. When someone leaves, we shut off their access the same day, so former employees aren't still holding keys to your email, files or accounts. A company password manager and multi-factor sign-in close the doors most small-business breaches walk through.

  • Same-day offboarding
  • 1Password for your whole team
  • Multi-factor sign-in
  • Account and access reviews

A network you don't think about

We install business-grade firewalls, switches and Wi-Fi in your office, shop or yard, link your locations securely, and monitor every site. When something drops, we get the alert and start working on it, often before you notice.

  • Reliable office network and Wi-Fi
  • Site-to-site VPN between locations
  • 24/7 network monitoring
  • Documented network at each site

Project files you can get back

Bids, drawings and project records are the business. We back up your computers, servers and Microsoft 365 data, and have a plan ready if a drive dies or ransomware hits.

  • Backups of project files
  • Computer and server backup
  • Microsoft 365 backup
  • Disaster recovery planning
