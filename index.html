Do you provide on-site IT support in Reno?

Yes. Founding Fathers IT is based in Reno. We come on site when a problem needs hands on a machine or a cable run, and fix things remotely when it doesn't, so you're not waiting.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Reno and support businesses from Carson City to Fallon and up to Gerlach, including Sparks, Dayton, Fernley and the communities in between. We work on site across that area and remotely anywhere.

What size businesses do you support?

Small and mid-sized businesses, especially construction and trades companies, professional offices, and businesses with more than one location.

How does billing work?

Every request gets a ticket, and every hour of work shows up on your invoice with notes on what we did. No mystery charges.

What does managed IT include?

A help desk your staff can call or email, Windows and Mac updates, new-hire setup and offboarding, software installs, printer support, and remote and on-site support.
