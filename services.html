Services

Everything your office needs to keep working.

Five services that cover the technology a small business runs on. Take one, or let us handle all of it under a single agreement.

Managed IT

Your team gets one number and one email for every IT problem. We answer, open a ticket, and work it until it's fixed, remotely when we can and on site when we can't.

Behind the scenes we keep every computer patched and updated, set up new hires before their first day, and shut off access the day someone leaves.

Includes

  • Help desk by phone and email
  • Windows and Mac updates
  • New-hire setup and offboarding
  • Software installs
  • Printers and peripherals
  • Remote and on-site support

Cybersecurity

Most small-business breaches start with a reused password or a phishing email. We close those doors first: a company password manager, multi-factor sign-in on every account that supports it, and endpoint protection on every machine.

We also review who has access to what, so former employees and old vendors aren't still holding keys.

Includes

  • 1Password for your whole team
  • Multi-factor sign-in
  • Endpoint protection
  • Account and access reviews
  • Phishing awareness for staff

Network design & support

We design and install business-grade routers, firewalls, switches and Wi-Fi, then document everything so nothing lives only in someone's head.

Every site we manage is monitored. When a link drops or a device goes offline, we get the alert and start working on it, often before you notice.

Includes

  • Routers and firewalls
  • Business Wi-Fi
  • Switching and cabling
  • Site-to-site VPN
  • 24/7 network monitoring
  • IP and device documentation

Cloud integration

We move your email and files to Microsoft 365, set up shared folders that make sense, and make sure everyone can get to their work from the office, the truck, or home.

We manage the licenses and settings afterward, so you're not paying for seats nobody uses.

Includes

  • Microsoft 365 setup
  • Email migration
  • SharePoint and OneDrive
  • License management
  • Domain and DNS

Data backup & recovery

We back up your computers, servers and cloud data, so a dead drive or a deleted folder doesn't mean lost work.

If a drive dies or ransomware hits, we already have the plan to get you running again.

Includes

  • Computer and server backup
  • Microsoft 365 backup
  • File recovery
  • Disaster recovery planning
