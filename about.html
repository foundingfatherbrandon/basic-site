About

A local Reno IT team that answers the phone.

Founding Fathers IT was started in Reno in 2026 to give small businesses the kind of IT support larger companies take for granted: someone who knows your setup, watches it, and fixes it.

Our principles

Four articles we work by

The name is a promise to build things properly from the start. These are the rules we hold ourselves to on every account.

Article I

Local

We're based in Reno and cover Carson City, Fallon, Gerlach and everywhere in between. When a problem needs hands on a machine or a cable run, we drive over. When it doesn't, we fix it remotely so you're not waiting.

Article II

Watched

Every network we manage is monitored. When something goes down, the alert comes to us, so we're often already working on it when you call.

Article III

Secured

Password management, multi-factor sign-in, patching and backups aren't add-ons. They're part of how we set up every client.

Article IV

Accountable

Every request gets a ticket, and every hour of work shows up on your monthly invoice with notes on what we did. No mystery charges.

How it works

Getting started is simple

No long sales process. A call, a walk-through, and a plan you can read.

  1. Assessment

    We visit, look at your computers, network, accounts and backups, and tell you plainly what's working and what isn't.

  2. Onboarding

    We document your setup, fix the urgent items first, and put monitoring and password management in place.

  3. Ongoing care

    Your team calls or emails us when they need help. We handle maintenance in the background and bill you monthly.
