Built for businesses that work in the field and the office.

Construction & trades

Your work happens on jobsites, but the bids, plans, payroll and email live in the office. We make the two work together, and we make sure a lost tablet or a departing foreman doesn't turn into a security problem.

  • Laptops, tablets and phones for crews
  • Shared plans, bids and estimates
  • Secure remote access from the jobsite
  • Fast offboarding when staff turn over
  • Reliable office network and Wi-Fi
  • Backups of project files

Professional offices

  • Microsoft 365 email and file sharing

  • Secure scanning and printing
  • Backups of client files

Multi-site businesses

When you have a shop, a yard and an office, or locations across town, IT gets harder. We connect your sites securely and watch every one of them from a single view.

  • Site-to-site VPN between locations

  • One point of contact for all locations
  • Documented network at each site
